# Privacy Policy

**App: Bodogen (ボドゲン)**
**Provider: Saiki Iijima**
**Last updated: September 8, 2026**

Saiki Iijima ("the developer") provides Bodogen, a turn timer and resource tracking app for board games ("the App"). This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

## 1. Collection of personal information

**The App does not collect any personal information.**

The App does not communicate with any server, and it does not obtain, transmit, or store information that identifies a user — such as name, email address, phone number, location, advertising ID, or device identifiers. No account or sign-in is required.

## 2. Information stored on your device

To provide its features, the App stores the following **on your device only**. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

| Stored data | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Player names | Display during turns; reuse in later sessions |
| Timer settings such as time limits | Restoring your previous setup |
| Resource definitions, counts, and change history | Resource tracking |
| Scoring rules | Automatic score calculation |
| Display preferences | Keeping your chosen settings |

This data is removed from your device when you uninstall the App. It can also be deleted individually from within the App.

## 3. Camera use

The App uses the camera **solely to scan QR codes**.

- The camera feed is used only to decode QR codes; no photos or videos are saved.
- Neither the camera feed nor anything derived from it is sent to the developer or any third party.
- Camera permission is requested only when you use the QR scanning feature. All other features remain fully available if you decline.

## 4. Sharing settings via QR code

The App can turn a resource rule setup into a QR code that other devices can scan. This does not involve a server: the setting values themselves are contained in the QR code, and scanning restores them on that device alone. No room ID and no account are used.

If you show a QR code to someone, its contents (resource names, point values, and other settings) become visible to them. Please avoid sharing a QR code while personal information is entered in fields such as player names.

## 5. Third-party services

The App does **not** include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs. No user information is therefore passed to any third-party service.

If any such service is introduced in the future, this policy will be updated and the change announced with the corresponding app update.

## 6. Network use

The App does not require an internet connection. Every feature works in airplane mode.

## 7. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from children under 13. By design, it does not collect personal information from any user.

## 8. Data security

App data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted. Please protect the device itself (passcode, biometrics, OS updates) as you see fit.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised in line with changes in law or in the App's features. Revisions take effect when posted on this page. Significant changes will be announced in the app's release notes.
